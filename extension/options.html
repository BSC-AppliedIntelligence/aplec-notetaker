Fred extension options

Both values live in Fred Notetaker → Settings → “Browser extension”. Paste them once.

Microphone

Chrome can only show the microphone permission prompt on this page, not while recording. Grant it once here so your own voice is captured; without it, Fred records only the other participants (tab audio).

Meeting chat

Unlike a bot, the extension is invisible to the other participants. So when you start recording, Fred posts a notice in the meeting chat. Fred Notetaker keeps a record of whether the notice went out, and the meeting's page warns you when it did not.

Fred also saves the messages and links typed in the chat after recording starts (Google Meet and Zoom; private messages are skipped, and Teams chat is not read). The chat panel has to stay open for messages to be read.
